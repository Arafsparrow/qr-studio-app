## ✨ Key Features

- **Advanced QR Code Generator**: Generate Link, Plain Text, Wi-Fi, Contact (vCard), Email, Phone, SMS, Location, Multi-Link Hub, Social Profile, Event, and App Store QR codes.
- **Real-Time QR Scanner**: Fast in-memory viewfinder scanner with laser line animation, zoom controls, and torch toggle.
- **Gallery QR Scanner**: Instant dual-engine QR code decoding from device screenshot and gallery images.
- **Multi-Link QR Hub**: Bundle multiple web links, socials, and portfolio into a single, mobile-friendly landing hub.
- **Custom QR Colors & Styles**: Full foreground and background color customization with gradient options and multiple dot styles.
- **Logo Integration**: Embed Telegram, YouTube, X, Discord, GitHub, or custom uploaded logos directly into the center of your QR codes.
- **High-Resolution Export**: Export ultra-crisp PNGs in 500px, 1000px HD, and 2000px 4K print resolutions.
- **SVG Vector Export**: Infinite lossless scalable vector graphic export for professional signage and print media.
- **Local History Management**: Search, filter, favorite, and export your generated and scanned QR codes to CSV.
- **Offline Architecture**: 100% self-contained application bundle with zero external CDN dependencies.
- **Multi-Language Support**: Seamless instant switching between English and Bengali across the entire interface.
- **Clean, Modern Interface**: Premium dark navy and purple aesthetic with responsive touch animation and Light/Dark modes.
