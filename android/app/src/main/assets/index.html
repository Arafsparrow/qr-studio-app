Frequently Asked Questions

How to generate high-resolution QR codes?

Go to Settings → QR Preferences and choose 2000px 4K, or tap "Download SVG" for lossless scalability.

How does Multi-Link QR Hub work?

Multi-Link QR embeds an interactive mobile landing page into the QR code so users see all your links at once.
