Frequently Asked Questions

Does AX QR Studio work offline?

Yes! All QR generation, styling, camera scanning, gallery image decoding, and history storage are 100% self-contained and work without any internet connection.

How to export high resolution 4K QR codes?

Select '2000px 4K' above the download button for ultra-sharp print quality, or tap 'Download SVG' for infinite lossless vector scalability.

How does Batch QR Generation work?

Paste a CSV list or upload a .csv file in Settings → Batch QR Generator. All individual QR codes are generated and packaged into a ZIP file ready to download.
