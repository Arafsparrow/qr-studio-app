Frequently Asked Questions

How to generate high-resolution QR codes?

Go to Settings → QR Preferences → Default Download Quality and select 2000px 4K. Or tap "Download SVG" for infinite lossless vector scalability.

How does Multi-Link QR Hub work?

Multi-Link QR embeds an interactive mobile landing page into the QR code. When scanned, it shows all your official links in one clean view.

Is my scan data private?

Yes, 100%. All QR generation, scanning, and history are processed locally on your device. Zero telemetry, zero external tracking.
