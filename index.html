Frequently Asked Questions

How to generate high-resolution QR codes?

Go to Settings > QR Preferences > Default Download Quality and select 2000px 4K. Or tap "Download SVG" for infinite lossless vector scalability suitable for banners and print.

How does Multi-Link QR Hub work?

Multi-Link QR embeds an interactive, mobile-optimized landing page into the QR code. When anyone scans it with their phone camera, they see all your official links and profiles neatly in one clean view.

Is my scan data private?

Yes, 100%. All QR code generation, camera scanning, and history storage are executed completely offline on your device. Zero telemetry, zero external tracking.

About AX QR Studio

AX QR Studio is a modern QR code creation and scanning application designed to make sharing information simple, fast, and convenient. Create customized QR codes for links, text, and other supported content, scan QR codes securely, and manage your saved codes in one place. With multi-link support, customizable designs, and high-quality downloads, AX QR Studio brings useful QR tools together in a clean and intuitive interface.
