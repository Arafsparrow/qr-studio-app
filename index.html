Frequently Asked Questions

1. How to generate high-resolution QR codes?

In the Create tab, design your QR code and scroll down to the Export section. You can choose between standard (500px), High Definition (1000px HD), or ultra-crisp (2000px 4K) resolutions. For infinite lossless scalability on print banners, tap 'Download SVG'.

2. How does Multi-Link QR Hub work?

Select 'Multi-Link' in the QR type carousel. Add custom titles and URLs for your links (such as Website, Portfolio, Store, or Socials). AX QR Studio bundles them into a fast, mobile-friendly landing hub accessible from a single scanned QR code.

3. How can I scan QR codes from Gallery?

Open the Scan tab and tap the 'Gallery' button at the bottom of the viewfinder, or use the 'Scan Gallery Image' quick action on the Home screen. Choose any screenshot or photo from your device, and the offline scanning engine will instantly decode it.

4. How can I save QR codes to my device?

After customizing your QR code in the Create tab, tap 'Save to Gallery' to automatically store the image in your Android device's Pictures/Gallery folder, or tap 'Download PNG' / 'Download SVG' to save directly to your Downloads folder.

5. Can I use AX QR Studio without internet?

Yes, absolutely! AX QR Studio is engineered with an offline-first architecture. All core features—including QR code creation, style customization, camera scanning, gallery image decoding, and local history management—work seamlessly without any internet connection.

6. Is my scan data private?

Yes. Your privacy is 100% protected. All scan logs, generated QR codes, and preferences are stored locally on your device in secure sandboxed storage. No scan data or private information is ever uploaded to external servers or tracked.

About AX QR Studio

Version 1.2.0

AX QR Studio is a modern and powerful QR Code creation and scanning application designed to make everyday digital sharing simple, fast, and convenient.

Create customized QR codes, scan codes instantly, manage your QR history, and share information effortlessly.

With advanced customization tools, multiple QR formats, personalized designs, high-resolution exports, and smart scanning capabilities, AX QR Studio brings everything you need into one simple and elegant application.

Our mission is to provide a fast, reliable, user-friendly, and accessible QR experience with powerful tools and a clean interface.
